# Jobs to be done

| When | I want to | So I can | Concept surface |
|---|---|---|---|
| I start a pilot with a new enterprise account | agree the objective, the one workflow in scope, baselines and acceptance tests up front | judge the pilot against what we agreed, not against what happened | 01 Pilot scope |
| A change request arrives | see every object it reaches and who owns each | ask the right people before build, not after go-live | 02 Change impact |
| Something blocks the change | put a name on it and record how it was resolved | stop blockers waiting on "someone" | 03 Blockers |
| The change is ready to test | run the agreed tests on the current revision and see failures with an owner | fix the right thing and know evidence is current | 04 Acceptance evidence |
| The approver signs off | bind their approval to the exact revision and evidence | never roll out something different from what was approved | 05 Approval and rollout |
| Users have been invited | see activation and adoption with the denominator, exclusions and unknowns | report a number I can defend and spot a lagging role | 06 Cohort |
| I hand the account over | give customer success the state, risks and adoption picture, and give product verified findings separately from hunches | the account keeps growing after I move on, and product trusts my feedback | 07 Exports |
