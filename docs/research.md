## The role (Deployment Strategist - UK)

Source: https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/conduct/15fa6334-638d-48ed-a6aa-ce13f4e07d9e (read through the
public Ashby job-board API for the `conduct` board; listing published 2026-06-16, GTM team,
London, on-site, full time).

What the posting says the role owns (paraphrased, quotes marked):
- "You will make sure Conduct lands and sticks." Join strategic accounts early, "run the technical
  deployment hands-on, and fully take over post-sale".
- Onboard "hundreds of end users", "surface expansion signals", turn "a single entry point into a
  multi-year partnership".
- "A direct line into product" to shape the roadmap.
- Build "onboarding flows, tooling, reusable playbooks — enough that customer capacity compounds
  without headcount scaling linearly".
- Looks for: 2+ years customer-facing technical work (deployment-heavy, forward-deployed or TAM);
  depth in enterprise tech/ERP ("No SAP background required on day one"); commercial instinct;
  a builder; comfortable saying "I don't know".

How this concept maps to the role:

| Posting | Concept surface |
|---|---|
| Lands: technical deployment hands-on | Pilot scope with baseline and acceptance tests; change-impact map; blockers with owners |
| Sticks: onboard end users | Synthetic cohort with activation/adoption, denominators, exclusions, unknowns |
| Post-sale takeover | Customer success handoff export |
| Reusable playbooks | Deployment playbook export generated from the gates actually used |
| Direct line into product | Product-feedback packet separating verified findings from hypotheses |
